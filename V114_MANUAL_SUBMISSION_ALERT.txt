KB Market Survey V114 - Manual Dealer Submission Alert

Commands
/alert_submit 10
/alert_submit 20

Behavior
- Runs only when a Telegram user sends the command.
- No automatic schedule and no automatic group alert.
- Uses today's date in Asia/Phnom_Penh.
- Counts GT and HORECA Kobo submissions together.
- One valid Kobo submission equals one report/outlet visit.
- Excludes Outlet Name បូកសរុបរួម and recognized spelling variants.
- Includes official dealers with zero submissions.
- Shows only dealers below the selected target.
- Sorts by report count descending, then official dealer order.

V79 LOCATION, SCORE MARKERS, AND PRODUCT EDIT
================================================

Changes
-------
- Adds Province and District columns to Outlet & Product List.
- Province, District, Commune, and Village are cached from GPS Location.
- Replaces the Ratings summary card with unique Region, Dealer, and Province totals.
- Summary cards: Outlets, Region, Dealer, Province, Very Strong, Very Low.
- Displays movement numbers directly inside map markers (for example 2, 6, 7, 8).
- Adds an Edit action to every product row for editor-token users.
- Edit supports Stock, Movement 0-10, and Key Issue.
- Viewer-token links remain read-only.

Railway variables
-----------------
MAP_PUBLIC_VIEW_ENABLED=true
MAP_VIEWER_TOKEN=<read-only token>
MAP_EDITOR_TOKEN=<different editor token>
REVERSE_GEOCODING_ENABLED=true
REVERSE_GEOCODING_BATCH_SIZE=50
PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://marketsurvey-production.up.railway.app

Open
----
Read only:
https://marketsurvey-production.up.railway.app/map?access=<MAP_VIEWER_TOKEN>

Editor:
https://marketsurvey-production.up.railway.app/map?access=<MAP_EDITOR_TOKEN>

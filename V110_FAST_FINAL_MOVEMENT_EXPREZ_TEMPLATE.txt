Final movement rule
-------------------
- Total Outlets = Kobo submissions minus exact Outlet Name បូកសរុបរួម.
- One non-summary Kobo submission equals one outlet visit.
- Blank and zero Movement Rate become 1 only inside final calculations.
- The strongest product in each comparison row receives final movement 10.
- Other products receive proportional final movement 1 through 9.

Raw movement rule
-----------------
/raw_movement preserves submitted values. Movement Rate 0 remains 0.
The summary/control outlet is still excluded from raw output.

EXPREZ Can 330ml
----------------
- Added to GT freshness/availability as product No. 14.
- Remains in the EXPREZ movement comparison row.
- Added to both own-product and competitor field mappings.
- Updated GT template row coordinates without changing the HORECA layout.

Performance
-----------
- /summary no longer downloads SELECT * from the very wide Kobo table.
- /export uses one workbook with Summary_Data and Location_Outlet.
- Wide fallback data is bulk-loaded once where it is required.
- Target generation time is around 40 seconds, depending on Railway database
  and LibreOffice/PNG rendering load.

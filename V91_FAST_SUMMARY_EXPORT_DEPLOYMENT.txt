KB Market Survey V91 - Fast GT Summary and Export

Changes
1. /summary GT YYYY-MM-DD creates the approved one-sheet Region/Dealer summary.
2. /summary no longer runs a full Kobo sync before generating when the date is already in PostgreSQL.
3. /export YYYY-MM-DD is registered again.
4. /export loads the Kobo wide table once, calculates all dealers in one pass, and caches unchanged results.
5. Repeating the same command for an unchanged date reuses the generated workbook.

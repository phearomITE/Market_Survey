KB Market Survey V86 - GT Summary Template
==========================================

Feature
-------
/summary GT 2026-07-25

The command now creates the approved two-sheet management workbook:

1. Summary
   - 65 configured dealers plus Region Total rows
   - submission and outlet totals
   - CB LITE NCP movement bucket
   - leading competitor and movement score
   - management KPI cards

2. Detail
   - outlet rows for dealers where a competitor leads CB LITE NCP
   - stock, freshness, movement and competitor lead
   - clickable Google Maps link when GPS is available

HORECA summary generation keeps the existing simple summary layout.

V80 MOBILE MAP PERFORMANCE
==========================

Improvements
------------
- Compresses API JSON responses with GZip.
- Mobile initially receives at most 500 outlet markers and 100 product rows.
- Desktop initially receives at most 1,200 outlet markers and 300 product rows.
- Removes all-product arrays from the initial marker payload.
- Loads one outlet's product ratings only when its marker is selected.
- Renders markers in small animation-frame batches to keep the UI responsive.
- Makes the phone filter drawer independently touch-scrollable.
- Reverse-geocodes records whose Province or District is NULL or blank.
- Keeps Province and District columns in Outlet & Product List.
- Keeps editor-only updates for Stock, Movement 0-10, and Key Issue.

Important
---------
The KPI totals remain calculated from all matching non-zero ratings. Only the
initial browser payload and rendered rows/markers are limited for performance.

The Edit button is visible only with MAP_EDITOR_TOKEN:
https://marketsurvey-production.up.railway.app/map?access=<MAP_EDITOR_TOKEN>

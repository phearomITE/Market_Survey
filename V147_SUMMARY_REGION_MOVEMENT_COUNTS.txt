KB Market Survey V147 - Summary Region Movement Counts
======================================================

Commands covered
----------------
/summary GT YYYY-MM-DD
/summary HORECA YYYY-MM-DD

Change
------
Every Region Total row now counts submitted dealers by their calculated final
own-product movement:

- <5: dealer final movement from 0 through 4
- 5 to 8: dealer final movement from 5 through 8
- 9 to 10: dealer final movement from 9 through 10

Dealers without a calculated movement are excluded from all three movement
counts. Zero counts are written as numeric 0 instead of blank cells.

Example
-------
R1 dealer scores: 9, 9, 10, 10, 8, 7, 6, 10, 10, 8
R1 Region Total: <5 = 0, 5 to 8 = 4, 9 to 10 = 6

KB Market Survey V119 - Fast Reports and Manual Alerts Only

Scope
- Based directly on Market_Survey(20).
- Does not modify HORECA products, CB LITE NCP, Kobo XLSForm, product mappings,
  report templates or Final Movement calculations.

Fast reports
- Removes the two possible 180-second Kobo waits from report generation.
- Uses locally synchronized PostgreSQL data immediately.
- Background Kobo synchronization continues normally every minute.
- Sends Excel before creating PNG.
- PNG preview has a maximum 15-second deadline.
- Uses faster PNG scale 2.8 and maximum width 4000.
- Allows up to four Telegram commands to be processed concurrently.
- Expected single /report completion is around 30 seconds or less under normal load.

Manual submission alerts
- /alert_submit 10
- /alert_submit 20
- Uses today's Asia/Phnom_Penh date.
- Counts GT and HORECA submissions together.
- Excludes Outlet Name បូកសរុបរួម and supported spelling variants.
- Includes official dealers with zero submissions.
- Shows only dealers below the selected threshold.
- Runs manually only; no automatic scheduled alert.

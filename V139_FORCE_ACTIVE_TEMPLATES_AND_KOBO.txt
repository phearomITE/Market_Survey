KOBO MUST BE UPDATED SEPARATELY:
1. Open the Kobo project.
2. Go to FORM or Settings and choose Replace form / Upload XLSForm.
3. Upload this downloaded file directly:
   C:/Users/User/Downloads/KB_Market_Improvement_XLSForm_GT_HORECA.xlsx
4. Redeploy the Kobo form.
5. Open a NEW submission. If an old browser tab was open, close it and open a fresh form.

The first screen will show only:
- Region
- Dealer
- Report Type
- Validate

Report Date, outlet information, products, Ring Pull, and final issues remain hidden until GT or HORECA is selected.

Railway deployment does not automatically replace a deployed Kobo form. The manual Kobo replace/redeploy step is required.

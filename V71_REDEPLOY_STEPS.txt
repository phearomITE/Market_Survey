KB Market Survey V71 - Export + Unified Final Movement

Main fixes
1. /export is registered again and generates the approved Summary_Data and Location_Outlet workbook.
2. Movement average uses only explicitly submitted positive scores 1..10.
3. Blank and zero movement values are excluded from the denominator for own and competitor products.
4. The highest raw-average product in each comparison group is raised to 10.
5. The same increase is added to all other scored products in the group.
6. Duplicate positive final ratings are resolved by raw-average rank, leaving one Movement 10 per group.
7. /report, /summary, and /export all read the same final aggregate movement.
8. /summary and /export use batched wide-table reads and a shared snapshot cache.
9. /alert_submit remains manual only; no scheduled dealer-submit alerts.

KB Market Survey V148 - Dealer Final Summary Status
===================================================

/export YYYY-MM-DD now produces three sheets:

1. Summary_Data
2. Location_Outlet
3. Dealer_Status

Dealer_Status columns
---------------------
Date | Region | Dealer | Status

Status rules
------------
- Submitted Summary: the dealer has a submission whose Outlet Name matches
  the configured final-summary name, including "បូកសរុបរួម" variants.
- Missing Summary: the dealer does not have that final-summary submission on
  the requested report date.
- All 65 official dealers are included, even dealers with no submissions.

Cumulative export fixes
-----------------------
- Summary_Data Member is the most frequent Member value per Region + Dealer.
- Location_Outlet excludes final-summary control rows.

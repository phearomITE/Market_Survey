V150 - /export duplicate product row fix

Problem
-------
The daily Summary_Data sheet emitted both "CAMBODIA Sport 300mL" and
"CAMBODIA Sport 300ml" because product-list deduplication was case-sensitive.

Fix
---
- Deduplicate daily-export product labels using normalized whitespace and
  case-insensitive identity.
- Keep the first official label, "CAMBODIA Sport 300mL".
- Prefer own-product aggregate data and support a case-variant fallback.
- Add a regression test proving that only one summary row is exported.

This changes /export summary presentation only. It does not modify database
records, raw submissions, outlet totals, or the movement formula.

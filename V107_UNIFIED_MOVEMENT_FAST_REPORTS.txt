V107 - UNIFIED MOVEMENT + FAST REPORTS
=======================================

Commands using the same final movement flow
-------------------------------------------
/summary GT 2026-07-25
/report CA6 GT 2026-07-25
/report CKS1 HORECA 2026-07-30
/export 2026-07-25
/raw_movement 2026-07-25

Business rules
--------------
1. Outlet Name បូកសរុបរួម is a summary/control row.
2. The control row is excluded from Total Outlets, outlet-type totals,
   raw movement, product availability and final movement calculation.
3. A blank or zero product Movement Rate is automatically movement 1.
4. Raw_Movement exports movement 1 for blank and zero values.
5. Effective movement =
       adjusted movement points / genuine Total Outlets
6. Final comparison movement =
       ROUND_HALF_UP(product effective / highest effective * 10)
7. Exactly one product in each comparison group receives goal 10.
8. Other comparison products remain between 1 and 9.

Performance
-----------
The Kobo wide table is bulk-loaded once per command instead of one SQL query
per outlet. /export creates the workbook directly and does not copy a large
report template.

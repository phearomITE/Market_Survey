Performance changes
-------------------
1. Every Kobo submission is flattened and indexed once.
2. Product field aliases are compiled and cached once per process.
3. Dealer filtering happens before product metric parsing.
4. Summary parses only the own product and three comparison products.
5. Full normalized rows are cached by report date for 180 seconds.
6. Duplicate commands cannot leave multiple timed-out report jobs competing
   for Railway CPU.
7. Daily export no longer reads the huge PostgreSQL wide payload table.

Synthetic 1,653-row benchmark on the complete product flow
----------------------------------------------------------
- Summary normalization: under 1 second
- Full GT/HORECA normalization: about 3.2 seconds
- Raw movement workbook: about 4.3 seconds
- Two-sheet daily workbook: about 1.3 seconds

Khmer PNG changes
-----------------
- NFC-normalize Khmer strings and remove hidden zero-width separators.
- Apply Noto Sans Khmer to every Khmer-containing report cell.
- Clear Excel theme scheme/charset/family overrides that made LibreOffice
  replace the Khmer font and split clusters such as គ្រប់.
- Docker build verifies that fontconfig resolves Noto Sans Khmer to a real
  installed font file.
- The renderer logs the matched Khmer font before LibreOffice conversion.

Business calculations, GT/HORECA routing, templates and final movement rules
remain unchanged.

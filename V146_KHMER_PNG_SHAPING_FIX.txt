KB Market Survey V146 - Khmer PNG Shaping Fix
================================================

Outcome
-------
- Excel output remains unchanged and readable.
- GT and HORECA report PNGs now normalize Khmer text before saving.
- Zero-width characters that split Khmer consonant clusters are removed.
- Every Khmer cell uses Noto Sans Khmer without an Office theme override.
- LibreOffice runs in a private, true-headless profile (no X11 DISPLAY error).
- Railway verifies Noto Sans Khmer during Docker build.
- PNG conversion is bounded to 30 seconds; Excel remains available if PNG fails.

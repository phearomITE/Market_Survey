Workbook sheets:
1. Raw_Movement
   Date | Region | Dealer | Product | Movement Rate
   One row per explicitly submitted score from 1 to 10.

2. All_Products
   Date | Region | Dealer | 57 product columns
   Each product cell is its raw average before comparison-group promotion.

Rules:
- Excludes blank scores, zero placeholders and status-only answers.
- Excludes final summary control rows such as បូកសរុបរួម.
- GENERAL uses the same outlet-type scope as the General final report.
- Existing PostgreSQL data is used immediately; Kobo sync runs only when the requested date has no rows.

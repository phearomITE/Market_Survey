Rules
-----
- Outlet Name បូកសរុបរួម is excluded from Total Outlets and calculations.
- Blank or zero Movement Rate becomes 1 in raw data and final calculations.
- Each GT/HORECA comparison row uses the same coverage calculation.
- Exactly one product in each comparison row receives final movement 10.
- Other products receive final movement 1 through 9.
- Kobo wide data is loaded once per command for fast generation.

V93 GT SIMPLE SUMMARY ONLY
==========================

Problem fixed
-------------
/summary GT YYYY-MM-DD could still reach the generic/legacy summary path and
produce movement comparison columns or a Detail worksheet.

New guaranteed output
---------------------
/summary GT 2026-07-25 now calls a dedicated GT generator and creates:

  GT_Submission_Summary_2026-07-25.xlsx

The workbook contains exactly one worksheet named Summary.

Included sections:
- Total Regions
- Total Dealers
- Submitted Dealers
- No Submit Dealers
- Total Submissions
- Total Outlets
- Completion
- Region / Dealer / Total Submissions / Total Outlets / Status
- Region Total rows

Explicitly excluded:
- Movement comparison
- Competitor products
- Product scores
- Member column
- <5 / 5 to 8 / 9 to 10 columns
- Detail worksheet

The generator validates the workbook before Telegram uploads it. If any legacy
sheet or field appears, generation fails instead of sending the wrong file.

V125 Fast Reports - Remove Map and Dashboard

Root cause of hanging reports:
The bot started a full historical Kobo auto-sync three seconds after startup.
That background task held the sync lock, so /report waited behind it.

Changes:
- Remove the full background auto-sync task.
- /report performs only a targeted outlet_info dealer/date sync.
- Add an 18-second hard deadline to the targeted Kobo fetch.
- Remove Telegram /map and /dashboard commands.
- Railway starts the Telegram bot only; no web/map server.
- Preserve report, summary, raw movement, export, alert, GT/HORECA, Excel and PNG.

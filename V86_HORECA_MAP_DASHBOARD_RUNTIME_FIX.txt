Required Railway variables
--------------------------
PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://marketsurvey-production.up.railway.app
MAP_PUBLIC_VIEW_ENABLED=true
MAP_VIEWER_TOKEN=your_existing_viewer_token
MAP_EDITOR_TOKEN=your_existing_editor_token

Important: the PUBLIC_APP_URL value must begin directly with https://.
Do not enter PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://... inside the value field.

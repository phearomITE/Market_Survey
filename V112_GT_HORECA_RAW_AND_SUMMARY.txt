V112 - GT/HORECA RAW EXPORTS + HORECA MOVEMENT SUMMARY
======================================================

New commands
------------
/raw_movement_GT 2026-07-25
/raw_movement_HORECA 2026-07-25
/summary HORECA 2026-07-25

Removed command
---------------
/raw_movement DATE is replaced by the two report-type commands above.

Raw export behavior
-------------------
- GT command fetches only GT submissions and GT products.
- HORECA command fetches only HORECA submissions and HORECA products.
- Raw submitted movement zero remains zero.
- Output filenames include the report type:
  Raw_Movement_GT_DATE.xlsx
  Raw_Movement_HORECA_DATE.xlsx

HORECA summary movement flow
----------------------------
- Uses the same management summary template and final calculation as GT.
- Own movement focus: CBL Pint.
- Compared competitors:
  Tiger Crystal Pint
  HANUMAN LITE Pint
  Vathanac LITE Pint
- Product Competitor and Movement Lead display only a final movement 10.
- Total Outlets still equals Kobo submissions minus exact summary rows named
  បូកសរុបរួម.

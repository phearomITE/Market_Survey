V109 - KOBO SUBMISSION OUTLET COUNT + FINAL MOVEMENT
====================================================

Exact outlet rule
-----------------
Total Submissions = every Kobo submission for the dealer/date.
Summary Rows = submissions whose Outlet Name is exactly បូកសរុបរួម.
Total Outlets = Total Submissions - Summary Rows.

One Kobo submission equals one outlet visit. Duplicate Outlet Names are not
deduplicated. For example, 4 submissions with 1 summary row = 3 Total Outlets.

Final movement denominator
--------------------------
Every GT and HORECA product now uses the calculated Total Outlets above.
Blank or zero Movement Rate contributes movement 1.

Effective Movement = Adjusted Movement Points / Total Outlets
Final Movement = ROUND_HALF_UP(Product Effective / Group Best Effective * 10)

One product per comparison row receives 10. Other products receive 1-9.

Applied commands
----------------
/summary GT DATE
/report DEALER GT DATE
/report DEALER HORECA DATE
/export DATE
/raw_movement DATE

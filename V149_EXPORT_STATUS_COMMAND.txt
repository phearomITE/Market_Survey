V149 - Dealer Final-Summary Status Export
==========================================

Telegram command:
  /export_status 2026-08-01

Output:
  Dealer_Summary_Status_2026-08-01.xlsx

Columns (exactly):
  Date | Region | Dealer | Status

Rules:
  - Includes all 65 official dealers.
  - Submitted Summary: requested date contains the normalized final-summary
    marker in Outlet Name (including accepted Khmer spelling variants).
  - Missing Summary: no matching final-summary row exists.
  - Normal outlet visits do not count as the final summary.
  - A date with no submissions still produces all 65 dealers as Missing Summary.

Also preserved in /export DATE:
  - Member is the most frequently submitted value per Region + Dealer.
  - Final-summary control rows are excluded from Location_Outlet.

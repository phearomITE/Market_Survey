V130 - Excel-only fast report mode

- /report sends the Excel workbook immediately and does not create PNG/PDF.
- /report_multi creates and sends Excel only.
- /report_today creates and sends Excel only.
- Keeps the targeted lowercase Kobo dealer/date fetch and fast single-dealer aggregation.

The 10-second delivery is a performance target. Actual time can vary with Kobo,
PostgreSQL, Railway cold start, and Telegram upload latency.

V136 Kobo GT/HORECA, metadata, and PNG fix

- Every report/export/summary/alert command reads the requested date directly from Kobo.
- Dealer matching is normalized locally after the date fetch.
- Nested Region and Dealer values are preserved in raw movement and daily exports.
- Region, Dealer, Report Type, Report Date, and Outlet Type are required in the Kobo form.
- Blank Report Type shows all product groups; GT and HORECA selections keep their routing.
- CB LITE NCP is an own product for both GT and HORECA.
- Approved HORECA beer, beverage, sport, and water comparisons are registered.
- Pint own products keep only Sale Status, Movement Score, Stock Status, and BBE/Freshness.
- /report sends Excel first, then generates and sends one PNG preview.
- PNG uses a private headless LibreOffice profile, avoiding X11 and profile-lock failures.
- /report_multi, /report_today, /summary, /raw_movement, /export, and /alert_submit avoid PNG rendering delays.
